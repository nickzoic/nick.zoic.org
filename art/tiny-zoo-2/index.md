Tube lens needs to be bigger than this to prevent the image from hanging out the 
sides of the lens.

Sensor is probably 1/2.3" which is actually about 7.5mm on the diagonal.
A 1/2.3" sensor has a crop factor of about 5.6x so a 35mm lens is a
200mm equivalent.  I don't really mind if there's a bit of a vignette since
I can crop in software so 25mm might also be okay.

These lenses have about a 10mm pupil so as long as they are mounted close
to the objective they should be okay.
There's also C-mount lenses with much bigger front elements.
